# AstroMosaic

## Sky photo workflow

**Live sky preview**

The sky preview for the current project and selected band, refreshed as eligible inputs change. Its completed overview supplies the effective grid for full-resolution detail and photo export.

**Photo preparation**

The working RGB color, exposure scaling, and brightness corrections shared by the sky preview, close-up detail, and photo export. Overlap corrections refer to the full resolved sky grid.

## Sky mosaic validation

**Sky preflight**

The check of a saved sky selection, its WCS and calibration, and the proposed scientific export format and estimated size. The result gives the selected panel count, cropped dimensions, and number of channels.
