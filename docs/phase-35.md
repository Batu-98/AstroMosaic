For this fixture, use at most `2e-6` absolute brightness difference between embedded and reloaded WCS, exact coverage and NaN locations, and at most `1e-9` degree angular difference between the source WCS paths at the center and four corners. ### M31 Two-Panel Merge With 20% Overlap

Retrieved a 40′ ESO DSS2 red cutout of the center of M31 at J2000 00h42m44.31s, +41°16′07.5″ from plate A1HR. The unchanged 2376 × 2381 input has SHA-256 `955d3a00620fd6c1e0594dab13e1d3677385ae5ffeb2b347e3c3602b3f46bc26`. Independently reprojected once with bilinear `reproject_interp` onto a north-up ICRS TAN reference centered at (10.684796987754515°, 41.26882455339882°), with 1.0077084879976699″/px square pixels. The scale is the geometric mean of the source's one-pixel sky steps at the center along X and Y. The derived float32 reference with NaN outside the archive footprint has SHA-256 `703450a1391444ff5f1af592c47b1feff2e2c4dfb5c7320b4dcea287be83ee22`. [ESO DSS data and attribution](https://archive.eso.org/dss/dss)

Split the reference on X into `[0, 1320)` and `[1056, 2376)`. Both panels are 1320 × 2381. The 264 shared columns are exactly 20% of each panel's width. Subtract 1056 from the right panel's `CRPIX1`. Set `FILTER=DSS-R` and use the common scanner brightness scale in ADU. Run `astromosaic mosaic` with the two panels, the reference center, `--size 2376 2381 --scale 1.0077084879976699 --tile-size 128 --assume-surface-brightness`, and a new FITS destination. The reconstruction gate uses exact binary coverage and NaN locations, brightness within 0.002 ADU, and sky coordinates within `1e-9` degrees at the center and four corners.

**Pass.** All 5,565,167 covered pixels matched the independent TAN reference exactly. The common strip contained 627,835 valid samples and its maximum brightness error was 0 ADU. Export WCS deviation was `4.08e-13` degrees. The renderer used 361 tiles and reserved approximately 17.1 MiB of working buffers. AstroMosaic Desktop opened the saved project, created a 510 × 512 CPU preview, and exported the same samples, coverage, and WCS as the portable CLI. The archived FITS, reference, and panels retained their hashes. Local deliverables are `m31-20-percent.astromosaic`, `m31-left.fits`, `m31-right.fits`, `m31-merged.fits`, and a display-stretched `m31-preview.png` under `output/phase35c/m31/`.

The 34 held-out stars gave median 0.00875 px, RMS 0.02488 px, and P95 0.03412 px. Recovered gain was 1.119976 and the held-out median background residual fell from 27.311 to 0.144 ADU on 97 samples.
